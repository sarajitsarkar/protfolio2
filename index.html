About Me

I'm a final-year B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student, currently wrapping up my degree at BPUT. Programming clicked for me early on — there's something satisfying about writing code that actually solves a real problem.

My main interest right now is full-stack web development. I enjoy working across both frontend and backend — building interfaces that feel good to use, and designing APIs that make sense. I'm also genuinely curious about AI/ML and how models can be applied to practical, everyday problems.

Outside of coursework, I spend time building projects. I believe that working on something real — even if small — teaches you far more than just reading documentation. I'm also continuously brushing up on my Data Structures & Algorithms and core CS fundamentals, because I think they matter in the long run.

I'm actively looking for internship and fresher software development opportunities where I can contribute, learn from experienced developers, and keep growing.
